Eskoolia

School ERP: Academics and Library modules

  • Company project
  • Two modules built

Eskoolia is an established school management platform with about 15 modules. As an intern at Sria Infotech I built its Academics and Library modules and contributed to the project’s knowledge-transfer documents.

My role
Developer on the team, responsible for two modules
Employer
Sria Infotech, App Developer Intern
Time spent
About one and a half months
Technologies
Django · Django REST Framework · PostgreSQL · Celery · Redis

Project summary

What Eskoolia is and what I did

The product
A multi-tenant school ERP. One deployment serves many schools, and each school has its own data, roles and permissions.
My contribution
I built the Academics module and the Library module, and contributed to the knowledge-transfer (KT) material that helps other developers learn the codebase.
What I did not do
Eskoolia is a large product that I joined, not one I own. The other modules, such as admissions, fees and examinations, were not my work.
Technology
Django and Django REST Framework on PostgreSQL, with Celery and Redis for background tasks.
Status
An existing product that the team continues to develop.

My contribution

The two modules I built

Academics

Foundation setup, staff assignment, a class timetable, a planning studio for chapters, lessons, homework and syllabus, and reports.

Section names as shown in the product navigation.

Library

Console, catalogue, acquisitions, periods and occupancy, issue desk, lost and damaged items, members, transactions and logs, reports and stock check.

Section names as shown in the product navigation.

What I learned

Knowledge transfer and enterprise concepts

Knowledge-transfer material

  • Helped write the material that lets other developers understand the codebase.
  • Traced how models map to database tables and how the roughly 15 modules relate to each other.

Concepts I studied while working

  • Multi-tenancy: how a request gets its school context and how data stays separate.
  • Role-based and attribute-based access control (RBAC and ABAC).
  • Celery and Redis background tasks, including scheduled jobs.
  • Database performance in an existing schema.
